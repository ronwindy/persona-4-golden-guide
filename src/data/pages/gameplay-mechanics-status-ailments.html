Gameplay Mechanics

Status Ailments

Status Ailments can be given to almost every character, except for bosses. I created this section for a quick reference because I can never remember what these do accurately.

  • Poison: Lose 10% of Max HP every turn. Damage dealt is halved. Cannot be KO'd from Poison.
  • Rage: Character will always attack a random foe. Damage dealt and damage received is doubled. Accuracy is halved.
  • Down: Character receives about 10% more damage. Evasion becomes 0. Occurs when hit by weakness/critical hit.
  • Dizzy: Lose a turn. 100% chance to occur when hit by weakness/critical hit while Down. 50% chance to occur when hit by an attack while Down.
  • Panic: Will do one of the following. Attack an ally, throw away money, or do nothing.
  • Fear: About a 50% chance to do nothing. About a 50% chance to run away, if possible. MC cannot run away.
  • Silence: Cannot use Skills. Enemies can waste a turn. MC cannot switch Personas.
  • Enervation: All stats and accuracy are halved. About 50-75% chance of failing to use a Skill.
  • Exhaustion: Lose about 10% of Max SP. Damage received is doubled. SP cannot reach 0 because of Exhaustion.
