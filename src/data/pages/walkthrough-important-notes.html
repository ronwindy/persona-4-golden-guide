This is a brief summary of everything to be aware of while following this walkthrough. These all are really, really important tips and tricks to follow the guide as precisely as possible. Not following the advice in this section will result in falling behind the walkthrough. You have been warned.

The three pillars
01
Match the arcana

Starting on April 18th, you must always have a Persona of the matching arcana as the Social Link you are hanging out with (except for Rank 1 and Rank 10).

This guarantees bonus points whenever you encounter that Social Link to max them out as soon as possible.
02
One dungeon, one day

This guide assumes that you complete every dungeon in one day. The easiest way is to avoid every normal encounter and only fight Golden Hands.

The hardest dungeon to do this on is the first dungeon.
03
Save very, very often

I recommend saving every 2 or 3 days, however I will be saving once a week while testing just in case I miss something and need to backtrack really far.

Use every save slot, just in case you mess something up to backtrack.
Money to have saved

Make sure you have lots of money saved. There are some expensive things you may need to buy, especially Personas. After the first dungeon, you should have at least 50,000 Yen, and you should have an increase of at least 50,000 Yen per dungeon.

First time playing?

If this is your first time playing this game, this walkthrough will have minor spoilers, but no major story spoilers. You have been warned.

If this is your first playthrough, I would recommend playing the game on your own terms first, before trying to 100% this game. Its not absolutely necessary to play the game first, but it may enhance your enjoyment of the game.

Fallen behind?

If you get behind on a Social Link, you can still move on and have time to max them all. However, you may miss a smaller objective on your first playthrough, but you can delay it until the second playthrough to complete the game 100%.

The challenge

This walkthrough is made with the challenge to complete as much as possible with one playthrough, so if you want to follow the same challenge, you have to stay as accurate as possible with your game.

Difficulty
Normal

This can be done with any difficulty, however this can be extremely difficult on higher difficulties. I will be playing on Normal difficulty, as I find the challenge just right.

Most importantly, just have fun and enjoy the game, because it's really good!
