Lady in White

The Lady in White is located at the Shopping District Shrine at night starting June 5th. She will trade Gems in exchange for Fish. The Gems she trades can't be obtained anywhere else. She isn't available during rain. Note that Courage Rank Reliable is needed to enter the Shrine at night. Here is the list of everything she trades.
