Fixed Floors: Magatsu Inaba 2 and 3, Magatsu Mandala 3 and 6

Notes: Must have triggered Good Ending Flag. Time will pass to 12/23 if completed by 12/22 or earlier, and will pass to 12/25 if completed on 12/23 or 12/24. Glorious Hands are marked as having 999 HP in analysis, but they actually have 9999 HP
