Miracle Quiz

The Midnight Trivia Miracle Quiz is optional content located under TV Listings. There are three stages, and each stage must be completed to unlock the next. Winning the third and final stage will earn you a trophy in your room.

Each stage consists of twenty multiple choice questions. A correct answer gives one point while an incorrect answer will lose one point. Whoever has the highest score at the end is the winner.

If there is a tie, then there is no winner. Here are all of the known questions in the quiz. Note that due to the nature of this quiz, there may be a few questions missing from this list.

If you find one not on the list, send me a screenshot and I'll add it to the list.
