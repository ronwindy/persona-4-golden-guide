Same day

When you replay an event, it will take place on the same day as you hung out with that Social Link. This means if the event has any variations depending on the day (such as characters wearing different outfits depending on the season), you will see the exact same variation.

Dialog options

You are allowed to pick any dialog option including those that are normally locked off behind Status Parameters, however no music notes will appear during the event.

Multiple routes

If a Social Link has multiple routes, only the route you chose will appear in the Bonding Album. To unlock the other routes in the Bonding Album, you must play through them in New Game Plus.
