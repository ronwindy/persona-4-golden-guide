Gameplay Mechanics

Field Mechanics

Saving
You can save at blue butterflies in your classroom, the shopping district, the entrance of the TV World, and at the calendar in the living room. There are 16 save slots to use. After beating the game, you may save your clear data. Loading clear data will activate New Game Plus.

Weather Forecast
You can check the weather for the next 7 days at any TV or by talking to the Meteorologist on the school roof. Weather is important in this game, and making use of this feature is important to managing your time effectively.

Fast Travel
By pressing Square, you can fast travel to different parts of an area, or leave the area quickly.

Night Life
In order to explore at night, you need to obtain a part-time job that occurs at night, then talk to Dojima. You can explore most places, some Social Links only occur at night, and you can find some Social Links to talk to at night to earn more points.

Bonding Album

The Bonding Album is a new feature not present in the PS Vita version. Once you max a Social Link, you can visit the Sofa in your room to access the Bonding Album. This lets you replay any Social Link event from any Social Link you have maxed (excluding Fool and Empress).

When you replay an event, it will take place on the same day as you hung out with that Social Link. This means if the event has any variations depending on the day (such as characters wearing different outfits depending on the season), you will see the exact same variation.

You are allowed to pick any dialog option including those that are normally locked off behind Status Parameters, however no music notes will appear during the event. If a Social Link has multiple routes, only the route you chose will appear in the Bonding Album. To unlock the other routes in the Bonding Album, you must play through them in New Game Plus.
