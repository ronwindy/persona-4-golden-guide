Victory Cry Cheat

Starting on June 24th, it is possible to obtain a Persona with the Skill Victory Cry. This Skill heals all HP and SP after every battle. The method used to obtained this Skill is considered a cheat in the community because it eliminates all of the challenge of the game. Use it at your own risk of being bored by having no challenge in the game.

Here are the requirements needed for this cheat:

  • MC's Lv is at least 23
  • Have a Ghoul and Ukobach with base stats. Can be obtained from Shuffle Time in early dungeons.
  • The current Fusion Forecast is a Skill Change
  • Have lots of Yen (at least 100,000)
  • Be patient. This will take a while.

What we need to do is simple. Fuse Kaiwan, and hope that Tetrakarn changes into something you want. Some good Skills to get are:

  • Victory Cry (the cheat)
  • Repel Physical
  • Brave Blade
  • Mediaranhan

There is a 1 in 4 chance of getting Tetrakarn to change, and 23 possible Skills it can change into, so a total of a 1 in 92 chance of getting the Skill you want. The later in the story you go, the more Skills Kaiwan will have, and the chance of getting the Skill you want decreases.

If Tetrakarn changes, but you don't get the Skill you want, fuse away Kaiwan and transfer that Skill that changed, and try again. After multiple Personas with changes of Tetrakarn have been fused, fuse them together and carry over the changed Skills to increase the chance of getting the Skill you want.

Rinse and repeat until you get the Skill you want. If you want more than one Skill, then fuse Kaiwan and pass the Skill you want to keep to a different Persona, so you can fuse more Kaiwans. Getting Victory Cry will take about an hour or so.
