Victory Cry Cheat

CheatAvailable from June 24th
Heals all HPHeals all SPAfter every battle

Starting on June 24th, it is possible to obtain a Persona with the Skill Victory Cry. This Skill heals all HP and SP after every battle.

The method used to obtain this Skill is considered a cheat in the community because it eliminates all of the challenge of the game. Use it at your own risk of being bored by having no challenge in the game.

The later in the story you go, the more Skills Kaiwan will have, and the chance of getting the Skill you want decreases.
The Loop
Wrong Skill?
  1. If Tetrakarn changes, but you don't get the Skill you want, fuse away Kaiwan and transfer that Skill that changed, and try again.
  2. After multiple Personas with changes of Tetrakarn have been fused, fuse them together and carry over the changed Skills to increase the chance of getting the Skill you want.
Want several Skills?

If you want more than one Skill, then fuse Kaiwan and pass the Skill you want to keep to a different Persona, so you can fuse more Kaiwans.

Rinse and repeat until you get the Skill you want
Getting Victory Cry will take about an hour or so.
