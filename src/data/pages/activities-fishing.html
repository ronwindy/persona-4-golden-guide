Fishing Minigame
1Pick the bait

Select the bait you wish to use. Typically, each bait can catch two fish, shown by small ripples or big ripples. If the ripples aren't the ones you want, reel in early and cast again.

2Hook it

Once the fish bites, hit ○ Circle to hook the fish. A bar appears: use the Left Stick to move the arrow left and right. The bigger the fish, the more resistance it has.

3Reel it in

Move the arrow to the blue area of the bar and hold ○ Circle. The longer you reel, the smaller the blue area becomes. Let go and it grows bigger, but the fish pulls farther away.

4Mind the red

If you hit ○ Circle on the red area multiple times, the line will break and the fish will escape.
