Gameplay Mechanics

Calendar System

Persona 4 Golden uses a calendar system for both story and gameplay elements. Some activities you choose will pass time, others will not. The flow of time is as follows: Early Morning, Morning, Lunchtime, Afternoon, After School/Daytime, and Evening. After School/Daytime, and Evening are the only two times you may choose what activity you would like to do. Generally speaking, if an activity leads into a cutscene, it will pass time. The exception to this is accepting a quest for the Hermit Social Link. Story events are always on the same day, and some activities are during specific weather or during a certain day of the week. The game takes place over roughly a year, so your time is limited.
