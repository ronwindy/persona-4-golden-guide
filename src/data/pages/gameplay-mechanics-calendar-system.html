Gameplay Mechanics

Calendar System

Persona 4 Golden uses a calendar system for both story and gameplay elements. Some activities you choose will pass time, others will not.

The flow of a day
1Early MorningPasses on its own
2MorningPasses on its own
3LunchtimePasses on its own
4AfternoonPasses on its own
5After School / DaytimeYou choose
6EveningYou choose

After School/Daytime and Evening are the only two times you may choose what activity you would like to do.

What passes time
Cutscene = time passes
Generally speaking, if an activity leads into a cutscene, it will pass time.
Exception
Accepting a quest for the Hermit Social Link does not pass time.
1
Fixed dates

Story events are always on the same day.

2
Weather & weekday

Some activities only happen in specific weather or on a certain day of the week.

~1 YearThe game takes place over roughly a year, so your time is limited.
