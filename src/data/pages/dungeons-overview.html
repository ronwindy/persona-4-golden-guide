Dungeons

This section covers everything you can find in dungeons. You will need to complete an investigation before you can explore a dungeon. Here is the legend for the bestiary:

Lv---Level
Arcana---Enemy's Arcana. This doesn't really affect anything in game.
HP---Hit Points
SP---Spirit Points
Phy---Physical
Fir---Fire
Ice---Ice
Elc---Electricity
Wnd---Wind
Lgt---Light
Drk---Dark
Wk---Weak
Str---Strong/Resist
Nul---Null/Block
Rpl---Repel/Reflect
Drn---Drain/Absorb
RD---Rainy Day
Floor---Floors listed are suggestions and not the only places they spawn. If the floor listed is N/A, then they are summoned by the shadow above in the list.
