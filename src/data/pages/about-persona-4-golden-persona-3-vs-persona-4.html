About Persona 4 Golden

Persona 3 vs. Persona 4

This section describes all of the differences between Persona 3 and Persona 4 in depth. The differences noted are only from a gameplay perspective.

  • Weather replaces phases of the moon
  • Social Links with Party Members grants special abilities
  • No timer on dating
  • No gift items for girls
  • Dungeons take place after school instead of evening
  • 5 Status Parameters instead of 3
  • Quests from NPC's instead of Velvet Room
  • Multiple dungeons instead of one big dungeon
  • No more fatigue system (Thankfully)
  • Must pay at entrance to restore HP and SP
  • No access points, have to walk back, use an item, or use save point on last floor of dungeon
  • Rare chests need a key
  • Direct Commands are now under tactics (Thankfully)
  • Persona evolutions are from Social Links instead of story
  • Down status does not cause player/enemy to lose turn
  • Skills that have multiple targets only need to knock one player/enemy down to obtain One More
  • Guard replaces Wait (Thankfully)
  • Only one physical attribute
  • Analyze is now trial and error process
  • Can only obtain Personas from Shuffle Time
  • Arcana Chance in Shuffle Time
  • Reaper is not on a time limit, New Game Plus only
