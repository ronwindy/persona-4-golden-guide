Information

First Day: May 13th

Last Day: November 1st

Activation: Talk to Adachi at Junes starting on May 13th

Location: Junes, Shopping District South

Ultimate Persona: Magatsu-Izanagi

Key Items: Adachi's Number (Accomplice Ending), Adachi's Letter (True/Golden Ending)

Notes: Social Link ranks up normally until Rank 6, then ranks up with the story. As such, a matching Persona isn't required past Rank 7. If you don't reach Rank 6 by November 1st, the Social Link can't be maxed. Social Link has 2 routes that splits depending on which ending you obtain. Social Link is available during After School/Daytime or Evening depending on the rank.
