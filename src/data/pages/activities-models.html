Models

Models can usually be bought from Marutake Hobby Shop in Shopping District North after completing Quest 11. Models bought from the Hobby Shop can be constructed at the Work Table in your Room. Placing all models of a specific type on the shelf will earn you a stat increase for every Status Parameter. This is the list of models you can make.
