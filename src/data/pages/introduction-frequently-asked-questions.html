Frequently Asked Questions

This section covers common questions you may have about the guide. If your question isn't listed here, it may be in the Walkthrough FAQ further down in the guide. If you still can't find the answer you are looking for, feel free to message me on GameFAQs or email me at Hurricanehaon@gmail.com.

FAQ1: What is the purpose of this guide?
This guide was made with the intent to compile as much info on Persona 4 Golden into one source. The two main features of this guide are a day-by-day walkthrough, and sections detailing different aspects of the game such as a bestiary, info on Social Links, and a fusion guide. While this guide is still a work in progress, the walkthrough and the most important sections of info have been completed, and should be able to answer 99% of any questions you may have about Persona 4 Golden.

FAQ2: What does the walkthrough cover?
The main goal of the walkthrough is to experience as much content of Persona 4 Golden as possible. There are a few aspects of this game that are only accessible in New Game Plus, so the walkthrough uses two playthroughs to experience everything possible. The things that are locked until New Game Plus is a new Persona Fusion and the Secret Boss. There are also some Social Links that have two routes (and one that technically has four routes), which requires a second playthrough to experience. If these aren't that important to you, and you would prefer to only have one playthrough, you can use the guide less as a day-by-day walkthrough and more as a passing guide and alter things however you need to obtain your desired experience.

FAQ3: Can I use this guide with the original Persona 4?
Maybe? This guide was made specifically for Persona 4 Golden, but many of the mechanics of this game are also present in the original. Most stuff in the walkthrough should translate to the original, however be aware that there are a lot of events, mechanics, and items that are exclusive to Golden. As far as the other sections of the guide, only some info is relevant to Persona 4 as a lot of things have been changed for Golden.

FAQ4: How accurate is this guide?
The walkthrough was created using a test playthrough to ensure accuracy. However, there are some RNG elements in this game that can cause minor deviations to the walkthrough. Because of this, I HIGHLY recommend to save often and in different save slots. Expect to make mistakes, and to have to reload at some point. Even with my extensive knowledge of this game, I had to reload multiple times during my test playthrough. For the other sections of this guide, most of the info has been checked but not all of it. There may be some small details that aren't 100% accurate, but there should be no serious errors anywhere.

FAQ5: Does this guide contain spoilers?
This guide contains gameplay and minor story spoilers. The second half of this guide also has the identity of the main villain revealed. While you won't see this in the walkthrough until their identity is revealed, you may accidently uncover their identity if you explore the info for the last few dungeons before you actually get there. So as a precaution, don't look at the info for the last three dungeons of the game. You have been warned.

FAQ6: I've fallen behind on the walkthrough. How can I catch up?
If possible, reload an earlier save to fix the mistake. If you don't have the save you need, then you can continue on with the guide and move things around as needed to catch up. Prioritize Social Links and Status Parameters whenever possible. There is some leeway on the guide, however falling behind may result in missing a smaller objective in the guide such as reading all books.
