Gameplay Mechanics

TV Channels

TV Channels are the extras you can't find in the main game. This section lists everything you can find and when they become available. In order to access the TV Channels in the main game, tap the screen twice to bring up the button to open the menu.

  • YBC: Contains Persona Music Festival Footage (Only in PS Vita version)
    • 2008 Pursuing My True Self (Beginning of game)
    • 2009 P3 FES (Beginning of game)
    • 2008 Mass Destruction (June 5th)
    • 2008 Reach Out To The Truth (July 11th)
    • 2009 Deep Breath Deep Breath (November 21st)
  • Arts & Edutainment: Learn the themes and symbolism of the Persona Series
    • "What are Shadows and Personas?" (Beginning of game)
    • "The Unconscious: It's Just There" (May 7th)
    • "The Unconscious: Beyond Space-Time" (June 4th)
    • "Archetypes: The Structure of the Psyche" (July 2nd)
    • "The Nature of the Shadow" (August 6th)
    • "Personas and Sociability" (September 3rd)
    • "The Truth About Complexes" (October 1st)
    • "Psychological Types for Beginners" (November 6th)
    • "Interpreting Dreams" (December 3rd)
    • "Deeper Into Jungian Psychology" (January 1st)
  • Inaba Public Access: Play Midnight Trivia Miracle Quiz
    • Prelims (August 13th)
    • Playoffs (October 6th)
    • Finals (December 26th)
  • Big TV: Watch Cutscenes (Unlocked as you find them in game)
  • PBS: Play Persona 4 Golden
    • Epilogue (End of game)
  • Tatsu TV: View concept art for Persona 4
    • Giants of P: Part 1 (October 6th)
    • Giants of P: Part 2 (October 6th)
    • Giants of P: Part 3 (End of game)
  • Yasovison: Listen to the Soundtrack (Unlocked as you find them in game)
  • Jacktoons: Watch Persona related previews (Only in PS Vita version)
    • Persona Hits (Beginning of game)
    • Persona 4 Arena (End of game)
    • Persona 4 The Animation (End of game)
  • Midnight Channel: Interact with Teddie (System Clock must be between Midnight and 1 AM. TV Channel Clock between 0:00:00 and 1:00:00)
