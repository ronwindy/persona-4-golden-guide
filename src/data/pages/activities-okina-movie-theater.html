Okina Movie Theater

Each month, there will be a new movie at the movie theater. You can invite a Party Member to watch a movie which will raise their level by one, and also increase three extra stats. Since Rise can't fight, she doesn't get the extra stats. If the movie is the Party Member's favorite, then they gain points toward their Social Link. Each Party Member can only watch each movie once. If the current dungeon hasn't been cleared, then Party Members will decline an invite to the movies. Here is the list of each movie and all relevant details.
