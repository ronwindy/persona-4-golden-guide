Strengths

MC's role is whatever you want him to be. MC's biggest strength is the ability to use multiple Personas. This makes him completely customizable to whatever role you like or need, and allows you to change roles whenever needed. Since Personas can be completely customized with enough time and effort, you can create broken Personas which easily makes him the most powerful Party Member.

Weaknesses

MC's biggest weakness is an instant game over if he is KO'd. A lot of people hate this decision, but I find that this is the only thing that gives the game some challenge, as I almost never have full party wipes. Since he is the most vulnerable Party Member, you have to be very cautious and prioritize his safety above the rest of the party. He also has a few smaller weaknesses. First, because he can switch Personas his elemental weaknesses change often. This can be easy to forget and may result in some bad situations when enemies take advantage of it. Second, fusing lots of Personas is required to keep up with the rest of the party, which can require a lot of money and time.

Strengths

Yosuke's role is a mixed attacker with both physical and magic attacks at his disposal. He has access to Wind and Hit/Evasion Skills, which no other Party Member has access to. His biggest strength is his stats early game. He is the fastest character early game and is a good back up healer when needed. His mixed attacker role is also very good early game, giving him plenty of ways to deal damage to enemies. He also is the only Party Member with access to Dekaja, which can be very useful.

Weaknesses

Yosuke's biggest weakness is he becomes outclassed during late game due to his stats focusing on being balanced across the board. While it is nice that he is versatile, MC does this way better than he does. Most other Party Members start excelling in a specific role, making them more useful than him both in dealing damage and healing. He also loses his focus on speed which allows Chie and Naoto to be about as fast as him, which loses one of his biggest strengths. He also has abysmal Luck, but that's the least useful stat in the game so not a big deal.

Strengths

Chie's role is a physical attacker with a strong focus on multi-target Skills. Her biggest strength is the amazing multi-hit physical Skills she gets. This combined with both Power Charge and Auto-Tarukaja makes her a damage powerhouse. Some other big draws are her ridiculous follow up attack which is an instakill, and her ultimate Skill which is basically Heat Riser for the entire party. She also is the only one in the party with Skills that increase critical hit rate, and in early game her Ice Skills come in handy.

Weaknesses

Chie's biggest weakness is her magic. While her magic stat isn't awful, her Ice Skills get outclassed by other Party Members very quickly. She also has low SP, which doesn't help. Her other big weakness is focusing on single targets. While she does a lot of damage to all enemies, she has a harder time mowing down just one enemy. On a lesser note, she doesn't have any party support Skills besides her ultimate Skill.

Strengths

Yukiko's role is both a magic attacker and healer. Her biggest strength is her magic stat, as well as her amazing fire and healing Skills. She is both the best magic attacker and healer in the party. She can deal massive damage, especially late game as well as easily keep the party alive. She also has access to Samarecarm, Divine Grace, and Mind Charge, which are all amazing Skills. On top of this, she also has the most SP of the party without much worry of running out. On a smaller note, she is also the only one in the party with fire Skills.

Weaknesses

Yukiko's biggest weakness is her terrible physical attacks, which isn't surprising. If she runs out of SP, she becomes very useless very quickly. Her other drawback is she has low HP. Her EN isn't terrible, but she is usually one of the first Party Members to get KO'd. While all Party Members can get KO'd easily if you aren't careful, it tends to happen to her more often than others.

Strengths

Kanji's role is a physical attacker with a focus on single targets. His biggest strength is his absurd ST, and the Physical Skills he learns. He can mow down enemies with little effort. He also has amazing EN and the highest HP in the party, allowing him to both cast Physical Skills for days, and take more hits than anyone else. This along with Power Charge and Matarukaja allows him to be a powerhouse. On a smaller note, he is the only source of Electric Skills for the party. While his MA is horrible, the Skills he learns allow Electric Skills to be a backup if he needs it.

Weaknesses

Kanji's biggest weakness is his terrible MA, which is to be expected. While the Skills he learns allows his electric attacks to be a backup option, it'll never be a good source of damage. He also has the worst SP of the party, which can cost you some SP items. His other big weakness is fighting multiple targets. While he can easily mow down one enemy, groups of enemies are harder for him to deal with. He also has abysmal Luck, but that's the least useful stat in the game so not a big deal.

Strengths

Teddie's role is essentially a backup Party Member. Teddie's biggest strength is that he can fill whatever role your party needs. Whether it's a physical or magic attacker, healer, or even support, Teddie can do it all pretty well. His stats are pretty good across the board. He also is the best with Ice Skills, as Chie can't do much damage with magic. On top of that, he also has access to Marakukaja, Marukunda, Samarecarm, and Dekunda, which are all amazing Skills. On a smaller note, he also has a lot of resistances which helps him take less damage than other Party Members.

Weaknesses

Teddie's biggest weakness is he doesn't specialize in anything. Other Party Members can do what he does better. This makes Teddie more of a backup Party Member, but not always on your front lines. He also can't really handle multiple roles at once. If you have him on physical and magic attacks as well as healing and support he is going to get overwhelmed very quickly. And also, his ultimate Skill sucks. Only use it as a last resort.

Strengths

Naoto's role is instakills and damage protection. Her biggest strength is her effectiveness with instakill. She can wipe the floors with enemies as long as they don't resist light or dark, and can also use Almighty Skills as a backup. As an alternative, she can instead learn every element and target weaknesses if you prefer. She also gets Tetrakarn, Makarakarn, Tetraja, and Shield of Justice, which is fantastic for preventing damage to the party. On top of this, she resists/blocks all instakill Skills, and has no weaknesses.

Weaknesses

Naoto's biggest weakness is her low HP and EN. She is the easiest to get KO'd, even at full health. She also can't do as much damage as other magic attackers which can make her less useful than other Party Members, especially if she can't use instakill. This makes her role as targeting weaknesses less viable than focusing on instakills and damage protection.
