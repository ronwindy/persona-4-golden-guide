Main Character

RoleWhatever you want him to be
Max HP585
Max SP372
Strengths & Weaknesses
Strengths
  • Biggest: can use multiple Personas
  • Completely customizable to whatever role you like or need; change roles whenever needed
  • Personas can be completely customized with enough time and effort: broken Personas easily make him the most powerful Party Member
Weaknesses
  • Biggest: instant game over if he is KO'd
  • A lot of people hate this decision; the author finds it is the only thing that gives the game some challenge (almost never has full party wipes)
  • Most vulnerable Party Member: be very cautious and prioritize his safety above the rest of the party
  • Switching Personas changes his elemental weaknesses often: easy to forget, and enemies may take advantage of it
  • Keeping up with the rest of the party needs lots of fusing, which can require a lot of money and time

Strengths & Weaknesses
Strengths
  • Biggest: his absurd ST and the Physical Skills he learns

Strengths & Weaknesses
Strengths
  • Biggest: can fill whatever role your party needs
  • Physical or magic attacker, healer, or even support: does it all pretty well
  • Stats are pretty good across the board
  • Best with Ice Skills, as Chie can't do much damage with magic

  • Biggest: doesn't specialize in anything

  • Can't really handle multiple roles at once: on physical and magic attacks plus healing and support, he gets overwhelmed very quickly
  • His ultimate Skill sucks. Only use it as a last resort.

Strengths & Weaknesses
Strengths
  • Biggest: her effectiveness with instakill

  • Gets Tetrakarn, Makarakarn, Tetraja and Shield of Justice: fantastic for preventing damage to the party

Weaknesses
  • Biggest: her low HP and EN

  • Can't do as much damage as other magic attackers, which can make her less useful than other Party Members, especially if she can't use instakill
  • This makes targeting weaknesses less viable than focusing on instakills and damage protection
