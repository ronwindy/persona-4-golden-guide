Gardening

Gardening is a side activity that allows you to harvest items for dungeons. You can access the garden at home, typically during the night. In addition to planting seeds, you can maintain the garden.

WhereAt home
WhenNight

Maintaining the gardenThis will pass time, but will reduce the time needed to harvest the plants, or on the day before harvest increase the amount harvested. This can also increase Diligence and earn points toward Hierophant and Justice.

If you wait too long to harvest, the plants will wither and you will lose the items.
