Persona Compendium

Fusion Forecast

Fusing Personas on a certain day may grant you additional bonuses. You can check the Fusion Forecast of the current day and the next day, so keep an eye out for the Fusion Forecast. It can sneak up on you sometimes. Note that if there is no Trigger, than the effect happens on that day every time you fuse.
