Tips And Tricks

Best Personas

This section details the best Personas to easily defeat any enemy that stands in your way. Here are the Personas that I recommend using to make the game as easy as possible. Do keep in mind that some of these Personas are so good, they may eliminate all challenge from the game.

Izanagi-no-Okami

This Persona is only available in New Game Plus, and it is easy to see why. The biggest advantage this Persona has is Victory Cry, which heals all HP and SP after a battle, completely eliminating any challenge the game has to offer.

On top of that, great stats and a Mind Charge plus Megidolaon is a very deadly combination. However, this Persona isn't in a first playthrough so chances are you don't have access to it quite yet.

Trumpeter

Trumpeter is a fan favorite and a fantastic support Persona. The best build on this Persona is Auto-Mataru, Auto-Maraku, Auto-Masuku, Heat Riser, Debilitate, and Dekaja. All incredible Skills that makes a pretty broken support Persona.

Helel

My personal favorite Persona. Helel has some good stats, and comes with Morning Star, God's Hand, Mind Charge, Arms Master, Salvation, and Repel Wind to cover it's only weakness. This is a great all around Persona for any situation.

Yoshitsune

Yoshitsune is widely considered the best Persona in the game, and that is because it has the best Skill in the game, Hassou Tobi. A devastating attack, and if followed after a Power Charge, no enemy stands a chance. Combine that with Arms Master. and it's quite easy to see why this Persona is a fan favorite.
