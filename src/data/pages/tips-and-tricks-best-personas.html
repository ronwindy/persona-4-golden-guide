Tips And Tricks

Best Personas

This section details the best Personas to easily defeat any enemy that stands in your way. Here are the Personas that I recommend using to make the game as easy as possible.

Do keep in mind that some of these Personas are so good, they may eliminate all challenge from the game.
Izanagi-no-Okami
New Game Plus only

This Persona is only available in New Game Plus, and it is easy to see why. The biggest advantage this Persona has is Victory Cry, which heals all HP and SP after a battle, completely eliminating any challenge the game has to offer.

Key skills
Victory CryMind ChargeMegidolaon
On top of that, great stats and a Mind Charge plus Megidolaon is a very deadly combination.
However, this Persona isn't in a first playthrough so chances are you don't have access to it quite yet.

Yoshitsune
Widely considered the best

Yoshitsune is widely considered the best Persona in the game, and that is because it has the best Skill in the game, Hassou Tobi.

Combo
Power ChargeHassou TobiArms Master
A devastating attack, and if followed after a Power Charge, no enemy stands a chance. Combine that with Arms Master, and it's quite easy to see why this Persona is a fan favorite.
