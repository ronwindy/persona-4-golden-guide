TV Shopping

Starting on May 29th, every Sunday you can check the TV in your room to watch the TV Shopping program. Every week there are two bundles of items, and you can order one of them. If you miss the program on Sunday, you are given until Wednesday to order. The package will arrive at home a few days later. In addition, you will be given Prize Stickers which can be mailed in for items, and is needed for Quest 10. For info on what these items do check the Item List section.
