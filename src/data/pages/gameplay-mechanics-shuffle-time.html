Gameplay Mechanics

Shuffle TIme

Shuffle Time is a minigame awarded after battle where you can obtain new Personas and other benefits. Shuffle Time has a random chance of appearing, with a higher chance of appearing from various actions taken in battle, such as hitting weaknesses and All-Out Attacks. Finishing the battle with an All-Out Attack will guarantee Shuffle Time. During Shuffle Time, multiple cards will appear with various effects.

How it works
1Win a battle

Hit weaknesses and land All-Out Attacks to raise the chance. Finishing with an All-Out Attack guarantees Shuffle Time.

2Draw cards

You start with one draw. Cards with a + Draw effect earn you more.

3Sweep Bonus

Draw every card to earn a Sweep Bonus: the next battle guarantees Shuffle Time with two extra draws, replacing Death, Judgement, or Persona cards you already own.

You normally start with one draw, and may earn extra draws through card effects. If you manage to draw every card, you will earn a Sweep Bonus.
