Gameplay Mechanics

Shuffle TIme

Shuffle Time is a minigame awarded after battle where you can obtain new Personas and other benefits. Shuffle Time has a random chance of appearing, with a higher chance of appearing from various actions taken in battle, such as hitting weaknesses and All-Out Attacks. Finishing the battle with an All-Out Attack will guarantee Shuffle Time. During Shuffle Time, multiple cards will appear with various effects. You normally start with one draw, and may earn extra draws through card effects. If you manage to draw every card, you will earn a Sweep Bonus. Sweep Bonus guarantees Shuffle Time in the next battle, giving you two extra draws and replacing Death, Judgement, or currently owned Persona cards. Here is the list of cards available through Shuffle Time.

Card List

Fool: +1 Draw, Changes all undrawn cards.

Magician: Rare Card. Changes a Skill of currently equipped Persona to a better one. If all Skills on Persona are the best Skill available, nothing happens.

Priestess: +1 Draw, Changes one undrawn card into a random Arcana card.

Empress: +1 Draw, Removes one undrawn card.

Emperor: Rare Card. Currently equipped Persona gains one level.

Hierophant: +1 Draw, Changes one undrawn card into a random Persona card.

Lovers: +2 Draw, No item drops from battle are awarded.

Chariot: Gives one point of AG to currently equipped Persona.

Justice: Gives one point of ST to currently equipped Persona.

Hermit: Shadows will not aggro. Effect ends upon next battle or leaving current floor.

Fortune: Gives one point of LU to currently equipped Persona.

Strength: Gives one point of MA to currently equipped Persona.

Hanged Man: Gives one point of EN to currently equipped Persona.

Death: Ends Shuffle Time.

Temperance: Gives one Chest Key.

Devil: +3 Draw, No Exp from battle is awarded.

Tower: +3 Draw, No money from battle is awarded.

Star: +1 Draw, Removes one drawn card.

Moon: +2 Draw, Exp from battle is cut in half.

Sun: +2 Draw, Money from battle is cut in half.

Judgement: No effect.

Aeon: Rare Card. +4 Draw.

World: No effect.

Sword: Gives one Skill Card. Skill Card given is based on card rank.

Cup: Restores party's HP and SP. Formula for this is unknown.

Coin: Increases money earned from battle. Formula assumed to be an additional 0.2 times card rank. This stacks with Sun/Tower card, meaning Rank 5 card will negate Tower card.

Wand: Increases Exp earned from battle. Formula assumed to be an additional 0.2 times card rank. This stacks with Moon/Devil card, meaning Rank 5 card will negate Devil card.

Persona: Gives Persona on card. Personas available are based on current dungeon. If Persona is already in deck, nothing happens.
