Introduction

Thank you very much for following this guide! This walkthrough will be focused on completing this game 100% in 2 playthroughs. Before we begin, check the Config menu to adjust the settings of the game however you like. And with that, relax and have fun with the game!

Difficulty

This walkthrough can be enjoyed on any difficulty. However, the higher difficulties can make it hard to follow this walkthrough if you aren't familiar with them. For my test playthrough, I played the game on Normal difficulty.

NOTE!

In every version except the PS Vita version, you can change the difficulty at any time and even customize the difficulty. Feel free to change difficulty settings as needed to complete dungeons in one day or to reduce the amount of time needed to grind for levels or money.

Advice

Outside of fulfilling certain objectives for 100% completion, this walkthrough will not focus on dungeons or battles. However, I will be offering advice as we go through the game to help you clear dungeons easily. To start, there are two basic types of attacks you can use.

Melee attacks, and Persona Skills. Melee attacks are weaker than other attacks, but weapons can be bought later in the game to increase damage. Persona Skills are more powerful, but will cost either HP or SP. Keep a close eye on your health, because if MC's HP hits 0, then it's game over.

Advice

Each Skill has a certain type of affinity, and most enemies are weak to certain affinities. Hitting an enemy's weakness, you will inflict Down status. When an enemy is knocked down, you will obtain a One More, and can take another turn.

You can chain multiple One Mores together as long as you keep knocking enemies down. By hitting enemy's weaknesses and knocking down every enemy, you can perform an All-Out Attack. This is a hard hitting attack of Almighty damage to all enemies, and will usually kill them instantly. Using All-Out Attacks can be a saving grace in an otherwise difficult battle.

This system is a crucial part of battles, and understanding this mechanic is important to deal with harder enemies later down the line.

MC has the power of the Wild Card, which means he can wield multiple Personas. Because of this, MC is the most versatile Party Member. However, keep in mind that weaknesses and stats change depending on which Persona is equipped. Enemies can and will punish you if you aren't paying attention to the Persona you have equipped.

You can now change Party Tactics. While the AI is decent, I highly recommend putting them on Direct Commands. It will save you from headaches later.

Shuffle Time

Shuffle Time is one of two ways to obtain Personas. Shuffle Time is obtained by exploiting enemy weaknesses, using All-Out Attacks, and finishing the battle quickly. If there are no Personas to collect, your priority should be something close to this: Sweep Bonus, Magician, Temperance, Hermit, Emperor. Getting a Sweep Bonus guarantees Shuffle Time next battle, gives you 3 picks instead of one, and gets rid of cards with negative effects.

Advice

From here on out, we need to finish every dungeon in one day. This can be tricky, especially in the early game. For this dungeon, we will use 3 Ball Lightnings to take out one Golden Shadow. (WAQ7: How do you beat a dungeon in one day?)

You can now fuse Personas. You may notice that MC's Personas level up slower compared to the rest of the team. In order to stay caught up with the team, you will need to fuse Personas constantly. You will also need to fuse certain Personas for Social Links. More on that later.

Because of how expensive things are in this game, we will need to get as much money as possible. The best ways to do this is to grind from Golden Shadows. We will need at least 50,000 Yen before we leave.

The Reaper

After opening 20 chests, a random chest on the floor you are on will contain The Reaper. He is a Lv 85 optional boss. I will note when I decide to take him down.
