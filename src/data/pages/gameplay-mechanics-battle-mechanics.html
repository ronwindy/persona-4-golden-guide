Stats
ExpExperienceAssumed

Used to level up. There is no way to confirm how the Exp system works as of this version of the guide, but here is what is assumed to be the Exp system.

Enemies drop a fixed amount of ExpSame level: same ExpLower level: more ExpHigher level: less Exp

Party members and Personas obtain the same amount of Exp if they are the same level.

LvCore
Level

Level increases after a certain amount of Exp. Level determines what Personas can be fused, and when Skills are learned.

HPResource
Hit Points

Used for Physical Skills. When HP equals 0, the Party Member becomes KO'd. MC will die if he loses all HP, resulting in a game over. A popular theory is that's his drawback for having the power of the wild card.

SPResource
Spirit Points

Used for Magic Skills. Nothing happens if SP equals 0.

STOffence
Strength

Base stat for damage done for attack command and Physical Skills. Damage done is assumed to be based on (ST/EN)* either attack or power of Physical Skill.

MAOffence
Magic

Base stat for all Magic Skills. Damage done is assumed to be based on (MA/EN)* power of Magic Skill. Also affects the amount of HP healed with Healing Skills.

ATOffence
Attack

Modifier on ST used for attack command. Damage done is assumed to be based on (ST/EN)*AT.

ENDefence
Endurance

Base stat for how much damage a character receives.

DFDefence
Defense

Assumed to be a modifier on EN to reduce damage.

AGSpeed
Agility

Assumed to be base stat for chance of hitting/dodging an enemy. Also seems to determine turn order.

HTSpeed
Hit Rate

Assumed to be a modifier on AG specifically for the probability of hitting an enemy.

EVSpeed
Evasion

Assumed to be a modifier on AG specifically to decrease the probability of dodging an enemy.

LULuck
Luck

Primarily affects critical hit rate. Also assumed to affect the following: dodge rate, hit rate, chance of being hit by Status Ailments. Unsure what else this stat affects.

Assumed damage(ST / EN) × AT(MA / EN) × Power

Escape

Attempt to run from battle. If you can't escape immediately, it takes a turn and you have to wait until the navigator finds an opening to run away. Not always a reliable way to escape.

TacticsGives instructions to Party Members.
Act FreelyDoes whatever they determine is the best move. Decent, but doesn't understand advanced tactics you may want to employ.
Full AssaultAttacks with most damage possible. Won't heal unless they have next to no health, and "usually" won't make absurd decisions. Again, "usually".
Conserve SPAvoids using SP whenever possible. Only uses SP in emergencies.
Heal/SupportWill heal, use status ailments, or buffs/debuffs.
Direct CommandsYou control what they do. Recommended unless you like the AI deciding what to do. This is the tactic I use.

All-Out Attack
How it works
1

After every enemy is Down, you can perform an All-Out Attack.

2

Everyone will charge the enemies and inflict Almighty damage to every enemy.

Can't be done when
!

MC is inflicted by a Status Ailment.

!

MC is by himself.

!

A Party Member is inflicted by certain Status Ailments: they can't participate.

Combination Attacks

After an All-Out Attack, a Combination Attack will occur if you meet 2 requirements: certain characters are in your party, and one of them knocks down the last enemy to initiate an AOA. A Combination attack is simply another AOA but flashier. These are the different Combination Attacks:

Social Link Abilities

Party Members will earn new abilities in battle on certain Social Link Ranks. Everyone has the same abilities except for Rise, who gets her own unique abilities. Here is the list of abilities:

Everyone except Rise
1

At the beginning of their turn, there is about a 25% chance of helping an ally with Down status back up to their feet. This will not take up their turn.

3

When MC knocks down an enemy, they have about a 25% chance to perform a Follow Up Attack. This will negate your One More. Each Party Member has a unique Follow Up Attack.

5

At the beginning of their turn, there is about a 25% chance of curing an ally with Panic, Fear, or Rage. This will not take up their turn.

7

They will survive a hit that would inflict KO'd status with one HP once per battle.

9

They will protect MC from a hit that would inflict KO'd status once per battle.

Rise only
1

Rise has about a 25-50% chance of increasing All-Out Attack damage.

6

Rise will inform you if you can currently target an enemy's weakness.

7

Rise will randomly provide one of the following to the entire party:

Heal HP and/or SP.Raise attack, defense, or hit/evasion.Cast Power Charge or Mind ChargeCure all ailments.
9

Rise will block an attack that hits the whole party if someone would be KO'd once per battle.

10

If MC is KO'd, Rise will revive with full HP and SP once per battle.
