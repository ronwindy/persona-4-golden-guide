Happy Fox Dates

Four costumes are obtained by petting the Fox when he is happy. This works on a set schedule of Happy to Neutral to Angry. In addition to this, these four costumes have the following requirements:

Kung Fu Costume: Pet the fox once beforehand, regardless of mood. Earliest day to pet the Fox is May 6th, making the first day to obtain this costume May 22nd.

Kingpin Duster: Available starting on June 21st after first obtaining the Kung Fu Costume.

Lord Flauntleroy: Available starting on July 12th after first obtaining the Kung Fu Costume and Kingpin Duster.

Track Suit: Available starting on August 27th after first obtaining the Kung Fu Costume, Kingpin Duster, and Lord Flauntleroy.
