Happy Fox Dates
Four costumes are obtained by petting the Fox when he is happy. This works on a set schedule of Happy to Neutral to Angry. In addition to this, these four costumes have the following requirements:
  1. Kung Fu CostumePet the fox once beforehand, regardless of mood. Earliest day to pet the Fox is May 6th, making the first day to obtain this costume May 22nd.
  2. Kingpin DusterAvailable starting on June 21st after first obtaining the Kung Fu Costume.
  3. Lord FlauntleroyAvailable starting on July 12th after first obtaining the Kung Fu Costume and Kingpin Duster.
  4. Track SuitAvailable starting on August 27th after first obtaining the Kung Fu Costume, Kingpin Duster, and Lord Flauntleroy.
