Shiroku Pub

At the Shiroku Pub you can work as a dishwasher to earn stat increases, as well as trade Gems for Weapons. This section lists the Weapons you can obtain here, and what Gems are required. Gems drop randomly from enemies, or can be traded by Lady in White at Shopping District Shrine. Note that Ryugu Stone can only be obtained by trading bugs with the owner.
