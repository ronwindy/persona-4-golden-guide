Bug Catching

Bug catching is a side activity used to help gather fishing bait. There are a few different ways to acquire bugs, and this section will detail all of them. The way to get bugs will change depending on what month it currently is. The three ways to get bugs are through the Bug-Catching Kid, using a net, and gardening during the winter.

Bug Net

Starting June 5th, you can obtain a net to catch bugs.

  1. 1Talk to Bug-Catching KidShopping District Shrine
  2. 2Talk to Lady in WhiteShopping District Shrine, at night
  3. 3Talk again to Bug-Catching KidShopping District Shrine
  4. 4Catch bugsShopping District Shrine

This involves a small minigame. You have to hit the X button the exact moment the ! appears above your head. The trick to do this is to watch the net, and hit X right when it hits the top of the fence in the background.

The better your timing, the more bugs you get. Bugs you get are assumed to be at random, but all of them can be used for fishing.

Gardening

During January and February, gardening is a way to catch bugs. It can be done once a night, and is assumed to be random on what bugs you get.
