Crane Machine

In Okina City, there is a crane machine that you can play. There are five grand prizes that can be won, which you can put on the shelf in your room. You can play five times a day, with the prize being won at random.

Days ending with 0, 2, or 6 give two plays for the price of one. On the 30th of each month, the chance to win a prize are increased. There are a bunch of items that can be won, but this section will only list the grand prizes.

New grand prizes are available on certain days, but won't replace old prizes. Old prizes will need to be won first before the new prizes will be available. Here is the list of grand prizes.
