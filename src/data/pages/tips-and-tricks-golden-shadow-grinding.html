Tips And Tricks

Golden Shadow Grinding

In order to complete dungeons in one day, a tactic is used that is referred to as Golden Shadow grinding. Golden Shadows drop a lot more Exp. than regular enemies, making them the ideal target to grind. There are different ways to accomplish this, however this section will list what I find to be the most efficient way to grind.

TargetGolden Shadows only
Hunt bySwitching between 2 floors
Yukiko's Castle3 Ball Lightning = 1 kill
Spawn rateRNG-dependent

NOTE! Every version of the game except for the PS Vita allows you to customize your difficulty. You can increase the amount of experience earned to make this process take less time if you wish. One thing to keep in mind is that the spawn rate of Golden Shadows makes grinding somewhat reliant on RNG, so the amount of time this takes varies based on how lucky you are.

Basic Strategy

The basic strategy is to only fight Golden Shadows, and to avoid all other battles (unless items drops from quests are needed). This ensures you level up your party without spending too much SP. To hunt for Golden Shadows, travel back and forth between two floors (ex. Yukiko's Castle 6F and 7F).

Never let an enemy advantage happen. While Golden Shadow grinding this is the most important aspect, as it can create some bad situations.

1DodgingRegular Shadows
  • Avoiding regular encounters takes a little practice. Regular Shadows have a hard time turning and will chase you in a straight line: run toward one and turn at the last second to dodge out of the way.
  • A Shadow will usually stop chasing if it swings and misses (if it barely missed, it may keep chasing), or if you run out of its range.
  • If you run out of its range, it stays locked on to you if you run back toward it, making it pretty much impossible to run past. Instead, smack it with your sword to avoid an enemy advantage.
2In trouble2 options
  • Smack it with your sword: this makes sure an enemy advantage won't happen. Obviously, avoiding fights altogether is the goal, but if you run into trouble it's better to get in a fight than get hit with an enemy advantage.
  • Hit Square to change floors and avoid the fight altogether. This is a very useful tool: don't be afraid to use it even if you haven't explored the whole floor. Staying out of trouble is much more preferred to exploring the entire floor.
3Regular encounter3 options
  • Run away. Extremely unreliable, and can sometimes take forever.
  • Use rush. Saves you from using HP and SP, however its effectiveness of mowing down enemies is dependent on the encounter.
  • Physical Skills when facing tough enemies, to conserve SP.

While exploring, make sure to pick up every chest you can. These give important healing items, and can sometimes have Golden Shadows hiding inside.

Yukiko's Castle

Once you find a Golden Shadow, make sure to chase it down and smack it with your sword. Always get as close as possible, as missing a sword swing is the worst thing you can do. Once in a battle, here is the strategy specifically for Yukiko's Castle.

Before enteringStock up

39 Ball Lightnings

Stock up on Ball Lightnings. You should buy as many as you can in a multiple of three. Bring in at least 30, although 39 is the preferred amount to buy.

In an encounterIn order
  1. Have your Party Members use Ball Lightnings. Three Ball Lightnings will kill one Golden Shadow.
  2. Once out of Ball Lightnings, use any other damage dealing items you may have picked up in the dungeon.
  3. If you are out of these items, use Physical Skills and attacks, and pray for critical hits.

Magic Skills are essentially useless against Golden Shadows.

After Yukiko's Castle
Go Physical

After the first dungeon, use Physical Skills and attacks to try and deal critical hits to Golden Shadows. Any Skills that increase critical hit rates are extremely useful, especially Revolution.

All-Out Attack

The goal is to knock down all Golden Shadows for an All-Out Attack, as Almighty deals a ton of damage to Golden Shadows.

Later in the game

Almighty Skills are another great option to take out Golden Shadows.
