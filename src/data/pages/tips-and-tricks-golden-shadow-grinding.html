Tips And Tricks

Golden Shadow Grinding

In order to complete dungeons in one day, a tactic is used that is referred to as Golden Shadow grinding. Golden Shadows drop a lot more Exp. than regular enemies, making them the ideal target to grind. There are different ways to accomplish this, however this section will list what I find to be the most efficient way to grind.

One thing to keep in mind is that the spawn rate of Golden Shadows makes grinding somewhat reliant on RNG, so the amount of time this takes varies based on how lucky you are.

NOTE!

Every version of the game except for the PS Vita allows you to customize your difficulty. You can increase the amount of experience earned to make this process take less time if you wish.

Basic Strategy

The basic strategy is to only fight Golden Shadows, and to avoid all other battles (unless items drops from quests are needed). This ensures you level up your party without spending too much SP. To hunt for Golden Shadows, travel back and forth between two floors (ex. Yukiko's Castle 6F and 7F).

Avoiding regular encounters takes a little bit of practice to get used to, so here are some tips to help. Firstly, regular Shadows have a hard time turning, and will chase you in a straight line. By running toward a Shadow and turning at the last second, it becomes very easy to dodge out of the way.

Most of the time, a Shadow will stop chasing you either if they swing and miss (if they barely missed, they may keep chasing you), or you run out of their range. If you do run out of their range, they will stay locked on to you if you run back toward them, making it pretty much impossible to run past them.

Instead, smack them with your sword to avoid an enemy advantage. While Golden Shadow grinding, the most important aspect is to never let an enemy advantage happen, as this can create some bad situations. If you get in trouble, you have two options.

First, smacking an enemy with your sword will make sure an enemy advantage won't happen. Obviously, avoiding fights altogether is the goal, but if you run into trouble its better to get in a fight than get hit with an enemy advantage.

Second, you can hit Square to change floors and avoid the fight altogether. This is a very useful tool to help avoid fights, and don't be afraid to use it even if you haven't explored the whole floor. Staying out of trouble is much more preferred to exploring the entire floor.

Also, while exploring, make sure to pick up every chest you can. These give important healing items, and can sometimes have Golden Shadows hiding inside. Now, if you do find yourself in a regular encounter, you have three options.

First, you can run away. The problem with running away is its extremely unreliable, and can sometimes take forever. Second, you can use rush. This saves you from using HP and SP, however its effectiveness of mowing down enemies is dependent on the encounter. Finally, if facing tough enemies, use Physical Skills to conserve SP.

Yukiko's Castle

Once you find a Golden Shadow, make sure to chase it down and smack it with your sword. Always get as close as possible, as missing a sword swing is the worst thing you can do. Once in a battle, here is the strategy specifically for Yukiko's Castle.

Before entering the dungeon, stock up on Ball Lightnings. You should by as many as you can in a multiple of three. Bring in at least 30, although 39 is the preferred amount to buy.

Once in an encounter, just have your Party Members use Ball Lightnings. Three Ball Lightnings will kill one Golden Shadow. Once out of Ball Lightnings, use any other damage dealing items you may have picked up in the dungeon.

If you are out of these items, use Physical Skills and attacks, and pray for critical hits. Magic Skills are essentially useless against Golden Shadows.

After Yukiko's Castle

After the first dungeon, use Physical Skills and attacks to try and deal critical hits to Golden Shadows. Any Skills that increase critical hit rates are extremely useful, especially Revolution. The goal is to knock down all Golden Shadows for an All-Out Attack, as Almighty deals a ton of damage to Golden Shadows. Later in the game, Almighty Skills are another great option to take out Golden Shadows.
