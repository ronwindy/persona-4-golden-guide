Persona 4 vs. Persona 4 Golden

This section describes all of the differences between Persona 4 and Persona 4 Golden. This includes most story differences, although they will only be minor spoilers.

  • New animated scenes
  • New songs
  • Updated graphics
  • 2 new difficulties
  • Can now fast forward cutscenes
  • Can now view cutscene logs
  • 5 new events
  • Festival dates now include Party Members
  • 2 new endings
  • Save points between long stretches of cutscenes
  • New character
  • 2 new Social Links
  • 2 new areas to explore
  • 2 more months of gameplay
  • Can now catch bugs
  • Can now garden and harvest plants
  • Fishing mechanic remade
  • Books remade
  • Can obtain models
  • Can now explore at night
  • Shiroku Pub at night; can now obtain gems
  • Lady in White shows up at night for trading gems
  • Hermit Social Link Rank 10 remade
  • Most Social Link Rank 9 and 10 voiced
  • 19 new Quests
  • NPC's with Quests are now marked
  • School questions remade
  • Thunderstorm weather added
  • Can obtain scooters
  • Can visit hot springs to learn new skills or relearn old skills
  • Can obtain costumes
  • Can visit the movies
  • Party member Social Link abilities changed order
  • Party member Social Link now earn new skills on even number ranks
  • Party members can use scooters in battle
  • Rise has new Social Link abilities: stronger AOA, buffs, cure debuffs, heal, shield fatal damage.
  • Party members have 3rd Persona evolution
  • Party member have combination moves used after AOA
  • New dungeon
  • No save point at end of dungeons
  • All enemies stats remade
  • Bosses have weaknesses
  • Buff/debuff extension
  • Revised shuffle time
  • No Mr. Pringle Trick
  • New shadow models
  • Golden Shadows remade
  • Reaper shows up on first playthrough, no longer New Game Plus only
  • EXP Weapons now in Shiroku Pub
  • New Personas
  • Search option for fusing
  • Can choose skills to carry over while fusing
  • Skill cards are brand new
  • More New Game Plus advantages
  • Online Functionality: Rescue Request and Vox Populi
  • Trophies
