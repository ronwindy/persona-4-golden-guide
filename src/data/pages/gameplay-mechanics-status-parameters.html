Gameplay Mechanics

Status Parameters

There are five Status Parameters that are exclusive to the Main Character. They have five ranks each, and they need to be at a specific rank to take certain actions, use certain responses, or to rank up certain Social Links. They can be ranked up in a variety of ways. Normally you earn 3 points for an action, but if a stat greatly increases, 5 points are earned instead. The Status Parameters are detailed below.

Courage

Points needed per rank:

  • Rank 1, Average: 0 Points
  • Rank 2, Reliable: 16 Points
  • Rank 3, Brave: 40 Points
  • Rank 4, Daring: 80 Points
  • Rank 5, Heroic: 140 Points

How to earn points:

  • Work part-time at hospital
  • Beef bowl challenge at Aiya
  • Read certain books
  • Ride the scooter
  • Defeating side bosses
  • Certain responses
  • Mabo Tofu at Aiya
  • Eating food in fridge (Greatly Increases)

Needed for:

  • Start Moon Social Link (Brave)
  • Start Fortune Social Link (Heroic)
  • To enter the Shrine at night (Reliable)
  • Certain responses

My opinion:

Courage is the easiest stat to rank up due to the huge variety of ways to earn points. It will almost always be the first stat you max. It isn't used for much besides certain responses during the game.

Sometimes the responses are hilarious, other times it doesn't do much. The only other problem is a chance of being behind on the Moon Social Link because you don't have Rank 3 yet, but should have Rank 5 for Fortune Social Link without a lot of trouble.

Diligence

Points needed per rank:

  • Rank 1, Callow: 0 Points
  • Rank 2, Persistent: 16 Points
  • Rank 3, Strong: 40 Points
  • Rank 4, Persuasive: 80 Points
  • Rank 5, Rock Solid: 130 Points

How to earn points:

  • Attend the sports club
  • Beef bowl challenge at Aiya
  • Work part-time sealing envelopes
  • Maintain the garden
  • Work at the bar
  • Chow mein at Aiya (Greatly Increases)

Needed for:

  • Hospital janitor job (Strong)
  • Origami crane folder job (Persistent)
  • Cast more lines when fishing

My opinion:

Diligence makes the hospital job really hard to obtain when it first becomes available. Otherwise, it doesn't pull too much of a threat as long as you keep up with it. Nothing really exciting happens with Diligence, but it's not the worst stat.

Knowledge

Points needed per rank:

  • Rank 1, Aware: 0 Points
  • Rank 2, Informed: 30 Points
  • Rank 3, Expert: 80 Points
  • Rank 4, Professor: 150 Points
  • Rank 5, Sage: 240 Points

How to earn points:

  • Study at Library/Desk
    • Extra increase from a book
    • Extra increase from rain
    • Extra increase at random
  • Getting a question right
  • Reading certain books
  • Beef bowl challenge at Aiya

Needed for:

  • Exam bonuses
  • Empress Social Link (Expert)
  • Fortune Social Link (Sage)
  • Translator part-time job (Informed)

My opinion:

Knowledge is one of the hardest stats to rank up due to the huge amount of points needed. The early exam bonuses are nearly impossible to obtain, but it can be done. Some of the exam bonuses are really good, so it may be worthwhile.

Starting the Fortune Social Link can be a little tricky, but if you keep up with it, it shouldn't be too big of a challenge. Empress Social Link is very lenient, so it's not a big deal if you don't have it immediately. Overall Knowledge gives the most trouble late game, especially for exam bonuses.

Expression

Points needed per rank:

  • Rank 1, Rough: 0 Points
  • Rank 2, Eloquent: 13 Points
  • Rank 3, Persuasive: 33 Points
  • Rank 4, Touching: 53 Points
  • Rank 5, Enthralling: 85 Points

How to earn points:

  • Helping a classmate answer a question
  • Work part-time translator
  • Work part-time tutor
  • Attend Cultural Club
  • Read certain books

Needed for:

  • Hierophant Social Link
    • Rank 2 (Eloquent)
    • Rank 4 (Persuasive)
    • Rank 5 (Touching)
  • Justice Social Link
    • Rank 4 (Persuasive)
    • Rank 6 (Enthralling)
  • Quest 12

My opinion:

Despite having the least amount of points needed, Expression is the hardest stat to rank up, due to the limited ways to increase it. Plus it is needed for 2 social links multiple times, making it hard to advance quickly through those social links. Don't take this stat lightly, otherwise you will regret it.

Understanding

Points needed per rank:

  • Rank 1, Basic: 0 Points
  • Rank 2, Kindly: 16 Points
  • Rank 3, Generous: 40 Points
  • Rank 4, Motherly: 80 Points
  • Rank 5, Saintly: 140 Points

How to earn points:

  • Work part-time folding origami
  • Work part-time daycare
  • Reading certain books
  • Beef bowl challenge at Aiya

Needed for:

  • Hanged Man Social Link (Generous)
  • Tower Social Link (Saintly)
  • Dishwasher part-time job (Kindly)
  • Certain responses

My opinion:

Understanding isn't too hard to rank up. Getting enough for Hanged Man Social Link can be challenging, otherwise Understanding isn't needed too much. As long as you keep up with it, you shouldn't have to worry about it at all.
