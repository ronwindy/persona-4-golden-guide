Gameplay Mechanics

Controls

These controls listed are for the PS Vita version of the game. With the PS4, Xbox, Switch, and Steam release these controls may now be remapped as you see fit.

Menu Controls

D-Pad/Left Analog---Move Selection

Cross/X Button---Select

Circle Button---Back

Square Button---Skill Order

Triangle Button---Exit Main Menu

L Button/R Button---Move Page

Field Controls

D-Pad/Left Analog---Move

Right Analog/L Button/R Button---Move Camera

Cross/X Button---Confirm/Search/Swing Sword

Circle Button---Back/Center Camera

Square Button---Fast Travel

Triangle Button---Open Menu

Start Button---Fast Forward/Check Map

Select Button---Log

Touch Screen---Vox Populi/Rescue Request/TV Channels

Battle Controls

D-Pad/Left Analog---Move Selection

Cross/X Button---Select

Circle Button---Cancel/Reset

Square Button---Persona

Triangle Button---Rush

L Button---Analyze

R Button---Turn Order
