Activities

Beach/Hot Springs

Once the Party Members have access to scooters, they will sometimes be available to go on a trip to the Beach or Hot Springs. These trips allow Party Members to either learn new Skills they otherwise don't have access to, or relearn Skills you decided to get rid of.

There are 5 new Skills each Party Member can learn, and they will always be learned in the same order. This section will list who is available on what days, and the new Skills they can learn.
