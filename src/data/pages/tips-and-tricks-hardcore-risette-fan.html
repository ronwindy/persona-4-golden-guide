Tips And Tricks

Hardcore Risette Fan

Hardcore Risette Fan is considered to be the hardest Trophy to obtain. In order to obtain this Trophy, you need to hear 250 of Rise's battle lines. This sounds extremely difficult, however it is manageable with some planning.

There is a lot of conflicting info on how this Trophy works. Thankfully, a lot of research has been done by the community and we can reasonably estimate how the Trophy works. This section will both explain how the Trophy works, and help you obtain it.

How it Works

The Hardcore Risette Fan Trophy is obtained after hearing 250 of Rise's battle lines. The lines that are counted are displayed in text during battle. Any line Rise says that doesn't have text is assumed to not count, which includes a bunch of lines from analyzing enemies.

The total amount of lines possible is estimated to be around 450, making this Trophy more manageable that one would first assume. This Trophy only counts unique lines. Hearing the same line multiple times doesn't count. These lines do carry over to New Game Plus, and due to the way PlayStation Trophies work, they also carry over between different saves. The Trophy is instead tied to your PlayStation account, and won't transfer between accounts.

NOTE!

The above only applies to PlayStation Trophies. I do not know how Xbox or Steam achievements are calculated.
