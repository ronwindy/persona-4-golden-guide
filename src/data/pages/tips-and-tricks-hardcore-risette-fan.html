Sounds extremely difficult, but it is manageable with some planning, and more manageable than one would first assume.

There is a lot of conflicting info on how this Trophy works. The community has done a lot of research, so we can reasonably estimate it. This section explains how it works and helps you obtain it.
How it Works
Counts
  • Lines Rise says that are displayed as text during battle
  • Unique lines only
Does not count
  • Any line Rise says without text (assumed), including a bunch of lines from analyzing enemies
  • Hearing the same line multiple times
New Game PlusLines carry over
Different savesLines carry over (the way PlayStation Trophies work)
Different accountsNo transfer: the Trophy is tied to your PlayStation account
NOTE! The above only applies to PlayStation Trophies. I do not know how Xbox or Steam achievements are calculated.
