Hollow Forest

The eighth dungeon. Has a lot of special rules.

Available: 2/13

Last Day: 2/13

Floors: 10

  1. Memories of Parting
  2. Memories of Pain
  3. Memories of Grief
  4. Memories of Sorrow
  5. Memories of Love
  6. Memories of Suffering
  7. Memories of Anger
  8. Memories of Loneliness
  9. Memories of Invitation
  10. Memories of Meeting

Fixed Floors: 4 (Sorrow), 7 (Anger), 10 (Meeting)

Boss Floors: 4 (Sorrow), 7 (Anger), 10 (Meeting)

Hidden Item: N/A

Notes: Must have triggered Good Ending Flag and maxed Aeon Social Link

Rules:

  • Items/Equip/Yen can't be brought into the dungeon. Any Items/Equip found in the dungeon can only be used here
  • SP is halved after every battle
  • Accessories heal SP every turn
  • Gold Shadows can only be found in rare chests
  • Can't use Traesto
