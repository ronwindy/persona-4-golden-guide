What Is Persona?
The Persona series is a JRPG series designed by Atlus. Persona is one of the many spinoff series of the Megami Tensei franchise. Other spinoffs include Devil Survivor, Devil Summoner, and Digital Devil Saga. The Persona series is easily the most famous spinoff, and is even considered more popular than the mainline games. The Persona series is commonly split into two sections: The old school games, Persona, Persona 2 Eternal Punishment, and Persona 2 Innocent Sin; and the new school games, Persona 3, Persona 4, and Persona 5. There are also several remakes and spinoffs, such as the Persona Q series, the Persona 4 Arena series. and Persona Dancing Night series. Out of all of the Persona games, Persona 4 is the most popular (Well, until Persona 5 came around at least). Since its creation in 2008, it has amassed the following: a manga series, 2 anime series, a remake, 2 fighting games, a dancing game, 2 Persona crossovers, a fighting crossover, a stage play, and even a pachinko machine. Persona 4 is a mix of a dungeon crawler and a life simulator, but the mechanics involved are expertly crafted in such a way that no matter what I say, I cannot do this game justice by trying to explain it to you. So if you have never played a Persona game, I highly recommend trying it for yourself. Its recommended to start anywhere you want as long as its Persona 3 or further. Persona 4 Golden is a remaster of the original game and contains about 50% more content. It was released for the PlayStation Vita in 2012 and is compatible with the PlayStation TV. It received a re-release on Steam in 2020, and a re-release on PS4, Xbox and Switch in 2023. Persona 4 Golden has a 93 on Metacritic and has sold over 1 million copies worldwide.
