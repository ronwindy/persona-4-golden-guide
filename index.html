APRIL

THE BEGINNING
4/11 (MONDAY)

Welcome to Inaba. Today is mostly composed of cutscenes and introductions. Sit back and enjoy the ride.

  • Arrive at Yasoinaba Station.
  • Meet Dojima and Nanako.
  • Stop by the Moelle gas station. (This interaction is very important for the true ending!).
  • Have dinner at the Dojima Residence.

NIGHTTIME EVENT

When watching TV in your room, you will experience the Midnight Channel for the first time. There are no crucial dialogue choices here, but it triggers the start of the main plot.

4/12 (TUESDAY)

Your first day at Yasogami High School. You'll meet Yosuke Hanamura and Chie Satonaka. Pay attention during class.

POP QUIZ!

Teacher: "What is the year before 1 AD called?"

ANSWER: 1 BC

+ Knowledge Up

After school, follow the story beats into town. You will unlock the town map. Head back to the Dojima residence when finished.
